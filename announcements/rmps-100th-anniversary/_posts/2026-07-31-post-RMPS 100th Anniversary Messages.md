<p>As Radin Mas Primary School celebrates her centenary, we invite students,
alumni, staff (present and past), parents and our partners to pen their
memories and/or well-wishes.</p>
<p>You may wish to write a personal note, or come together as a class, group
of friends or members of a club, activity or CCA to share your memories
with us.</p>
<p>Please share your well wishes for the school, memories of your schooling
days, and/or the words of a teacher who has made a positive impact on you.
We hope you can also include a photo of yourself, the school and/or a memento
that is meaningful to you and our school.</p>
<p>Please note messages and images shared may be used in our school publications,
including our website and official school social media accounts.</p>
<p>We look forward to reading your messages.</p>
