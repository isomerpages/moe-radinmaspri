<p>As part of Radin Mas Primary School’s 100th Anniversary celebrations,
the Radin Mas Primary School Alumni will be hosting a special Alumni Gala
Dinner on Sunday, 22 November 2026.</p>
<p>Join us as we gather across generations to celebrate 100 years of memories,
friendships, and shared experiences that have shaped who we are today.
Reconnect with old classmates, and possibly your teachers too, relive treasured
moments, and create new memories together as we commemorate this once-in-a-century
milestone. ❤️</p>
<p>Adding to the celebrations, the Radin Mas Primary School Alumni has also
launched an exclusive 100th Anniversary Pin Set, featuring both the current
school crest and the original “Gung Ho” crest. This limited-edition keepsake
celebrates our rich heritage and offers alumni, students, parents, teachers,
and supporters a meaningful way to own a piece of RMPS history.</p>
<p>📅 Date: Sunday, 22 November 2026</p>
<p>🕕 Time: 6.00pm – 10.00pm</p>
<p>🎟️ Dinner Ticket: $100 per person</p>
<p>📌 100th Anniversary Pin Set</p>
<p>💰 Price: $25 per set (inclusive of local delivery)</p>
<p>Gather your old classmates and form your tables—we look forward to welcoming
you home to celebrate this special occasion together.</p>
<p>For more information on the Alumni Gala Dinner and to purchase the commemorative
pin set, please visit <a href="http://www.rmps-alumni.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">www.rmps-alumni.sg</a>
</p>
